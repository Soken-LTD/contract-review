Security

This page describes how to report security vulnerabilities in the Soken Contract Review connector and its infrastructure.

Scope

The following are in scope for security reports:

  • The MCP server at mcp.soken.dev
  • The hosted report pages at soken.dev/r/*
  • The upload endpoint at soken.dev/u/*
  • The claim and verify pages on soken.dev
  • The public source repositories: Soken-LTD/contract-review and Soken-LTD/smart-contract-audits

Out of scope

  • Vulnerabilities in third-party smart contracts that the Service reviews. Those should be reported to the contract owner, not to us.
  • Findings in review reports. Reports are automated output of the requester's language-model session, not statements by SOKEN LTD. If a report contains inaccurate findings, see the takedown page.

How to report

Email: hello@soken.dev

Please include:

  • A description of the vulnerability and its potential impact.
  • Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept if you have one.
  • The affected component (server, hosted pages, upload endpoint, repository).

If possible, encrypt your report using the PGP key published at /.well-known/security.txt (when available).

Response

We aim to acknowledge your report within 3 business days and to triage it within 7 business days. We will keep you informed of our progress.

Safe harbour

If you make a good-faith effort to find and report a vulnerability in the systems listed above, we will not pursue legal action against you for your research. We ask that you:

  • Do not access, modify, or delete data belonging to other users.
  • Do not degrade the availability of the Service for others.
  • Do not publicly disclose the vulnerability before we have had a reasonable opportunity to address it.
  • Report the issue to us before sharing it with anyone else.

Contact

Security reports: hello@soken.dev
General support: hello@soken.dev

See also: /.well-known/security.txt
